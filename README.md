## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** designed to transform employee data into meaningful and easy-to-understand workforce insights.

The dashboard allows users to explore employee information through interactive filters, KPI cards, charts, and a detailed employee records table.

### 🎯 Main Objectives

- Analyze employee workforce distribution
- Monitor employee attrition
- Compare departments and job roles
- Analyze average monthly income
- Understand job satisfaction
- Explore overtime patterns
- Analyze employee age distribution
- Provide an interactive employee-level data view

---

## ✨ Dashboard Features

### 🔎 Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard by:

- 🏢 Department
- 💼 Job Role
- 👤 Gender
- 🚪 Attrition
- ⏰ Overtime

A **Reset Filters** button is also available to quickly restore the complete dataset.

---

## 📊 KPI Cards

The dashboard provides real-time KPI cards for:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| 👥 Total Employees | Number of employees in the selected data |
| 🚪 Attrition Rate | Percentage of employees who left |
| 💰 Average Monthly Income | Average employee monthly income |
| ⭐ Average Job Satisfaction | Average employee satisfaction score |

## 📈 Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

### 🏢 Employees by Department
Shows the distribution of employees across different departments.

### 🚪 Attrition Overview
Visualizes employees who stayed versus employees who left.

### 👤 Workforce by Gender
Displays workforce distribution by gender.

### 💼 Average Income by Department
Compares average employee income across departments.

### ⭐ Job Satisfaction by Department
Shows average job satisfaction for each department.

### 📊 Employees by Job Role
Displays employee distribution across different job roles.

### ⏰ Overtime Distribution
Shows employees working overtime versus those who do not.

### 📈 Age Distribution
Groups employees into different age ranges for workforce analysis.
